Evaluation questions

Does a trial automatically charge me?

No. This request does not create a subscription or collect a card. A paid arrangement requires a separate quote and explicit agreement.

Can I use real borrower data in the demo?

No. The public demo and free institution trial use synthetic samples only. Real institution data is a separate paid-pilot gate requiring documented authority, minimum necessary fields, secure handling and approved hosting, transfer, retention and deletion terms.

What determines the price?

The private quote shows the total pilot cost, likely production subscription, separate implementation/connectors/training, support or AMC, taxes and conversion credit before acceptance. Ordinary hosted support is included; no duplicate hosted AMC. Production prices are not introduced unexpectedly after the pilot.

Does Nepal validation establish readiness in India or UAE?

Each country and actual vendor/version needs its own configuration, permissions and conformance review. Expansion follows Nepal, India, UAE, wider SAARC and other validated markets.
