A separate institutional workspace, with deliberate access

The SaaS design includes server authentication, institution and branch permissions, audit records and controlled support access. These controls are being implemented and independently checked before production onboarding.

  • Strict institution data separation, with branch and role scope.
  • Temporary support access only with institution approval, scope, expiry and an audit record.
  • Country-specific hosting approved by the institution.
  • Production identity and security review before real borrower or financial data.

Questions before a pilot

Does CBSRM replace our CBS or MIS?

No. It is intended to work alongside them as a risk analysis and decision-support layer. Transactions and authoritative customer records stay in the source system.

Can any partner sell or maintain the platform?

Partners can work under agreed licensing, commission and AMC terms. Each institution is licensed separately; first-line partner support escalates to CBSRM for platform support and updates.

Can support staff see borrower data?

Commercial or support roles do not confer routine data access. Any temporary access requires institution approval, a defined scope and expiry, revocation and an audit record.

Is every CBS/MIS vendor already connected?

No. The framework is vendor-neutral. Actual compatibility must be verified for the specific product and version with its approved interface or sandbox.

Which languages and countries are supported?

The microfinance experience is being developed in English, Nepali and Hindi. Interface language is independent of country regulation and currency. Each analysis needs a compatible country and policy pack.

Does it make loan decisions automatically?

The current approach is explainable measurement and workflow support. Institutional lending and risk decisions remain with authorized human reviewers.

Is the AI assistant a deployed machine-learning model?

No. Transparent rules are not labelled as machine learning. Future local AI is a disabled extension until separately approved, and prediction models require lawful labelled history and real validation.

How much time will it save?

The pilot will measure manual steps and elapsed time using the institution’s process. We will not promise a percentage before a comparable benchmark is observed.
