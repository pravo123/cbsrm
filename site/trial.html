Before you commit
Paid pilot available. Pricing on request.
A qualified institution receives the complete pricing schedule privately before accepting a pilot: total pilot cost, likely production subscription, implementation, connectors, training, support/AMC, taxes and conversion credit. The 90-day pilot uses agreed kickoff/readout milestones and starts only after accepted inputs, signed scope/data terms and cleared advance.
Free trial: 14 days, synthetic-only, no card or automatic charge. Real-data uploads and production integrations are disabled. Existing research/Desk access and its separate trial policy do not activate a microfinance institution.
Security and integration features remain under acceptance review. Production access and tested institutional SSO are enabled only after their actual readiness gates pass.
